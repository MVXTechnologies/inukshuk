Maps, fixed.

• Large maps whose page is one big photo now render instead of failing with "Load failed". They also render far faster.
• A map imported before an earlier fix is corrected automatically on launch, including sheets that drew upside down — no need to re-import.
• Your position marker always stays on top of your maps.
• Importing a very large map no longer runs out of memory.
• Maps prepare themselves right after import, so the Map tab opens with them ready.
• Pauses in a recording no longer add the distance you travelled while paused, and time the app spent closed while paused is no longer counted.
