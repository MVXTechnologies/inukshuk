Inukshuk is kept alive by donations.

It is free for everyone, with no ads and no tracking, and it will stay that way. It is built by one person in Québec City and paid for by the people who use it. Every gift, small or large, keeps it going.

What donations pay for

Donations pay for the time and tools it takes to keep Inukshuk running and free. They are funded in order: first keeping the app up, then new features.

1. Keep the app up

  • Map servers and data
  • App store accounts and licences
  • Test devices and software

2. Implement new features

  • Developer time
