Inukshuk is kept alive by donations.

It is free for everyone, with no ads and no tracking, and it will stay that way. It is built by one person in Québec City and paid for by the people who use it. If everyone reading this gave the price of a coffee, the year would be covered in a day.

What it costs to run

Every dollar is listed here. Nothing goes to ads, marketing or salaries. The map tiles, trails and contours run on a home server and a free Cloudflare plan to keep the bill small.

Apple developer account $99 / year
Strava connection (API) $14 / month
Servers, maintenance, licences (cushion) $1,000 / year
Google Play account paid once
