# Inukshuk: UI design review

_Reviewer: senior product/UI designer (outdoor and map-first apps). Date: 2026-09-25._
_Scope: iOS 6.9" store set (Sep 2026), Android store and Play shots (Jun–Jul 2026, older build), and the design-system and screen code in `src/ui/*`, `app/(tabs)/*`, `src/features/*`. I only read the repo and changed nothing._

---

## 0. TL;DR

Inukshuk's engineering is careful. It has contrast unit tests, a11y labels on most controls, keep-awake while recording, honest empty states, and a well-designed offline-download sheet. The **UI layer has not kept up with that engineering.** Three problems stand out:

1. **The brand doesn't reach the screen.** The theme overrides the MD3 base roles but inherits Material's default **lavender/plum elevation surfaces**. As a result, the tab bar, every map FAB, the compass, the trail header card and the search field show up in Google-purple instead of stone, sage and paper. The Inukshuk identity only appears on the waypoint pin.
2. **The most important job is the hardest to do.** Recording a track takes two taps into a "+" sheet in the **top-right corner**, the worst reach zone for one-handed use. The live HUD is a 200 px card with 14 pt numbers, and its status colours measure **1.4–1.6 : 1**.
3. **The design system is several systems.** It has 6 MD3 themes (3 "app styles" × light/dark), plus `edgePill`, `weatherChrome` and `mapColors`, plus ~70 hard-coded hex/rgba values in 25 files and 56 raw `fontSize` values from 8 to 19 px. The contrast test only checks `onX`-on-`X` pairs, so the pastel "tertiary used as ink" failure goes undetected.

Proposals, ranked:

| #   | Proposal                                                                                     | Kind                    | Effort           | Impact             |
| --- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------- | ---------------- | ------------------ |
| 1   | **Trail Mode**: a thumb-zone record button and a glanceable instrument strip                 | Map / HUD               | M                | Very high          |
| 2   | **Stone & Paper tokens**: kill the purple leak, one semantic token layer, real brand moments | Design system / brand   | M (S first step) | High, every screen |
| 3   | **Sun, Night & Gloves**: an outdoor-conditions and accessibility pass                        | Accessibility / outdoor | M                | High (safety)      |
| 4   | **Library as a logbook, plus honest tabs**                                                   | IA / flows              | M–L              | Medium-high        |

## 2. Critique by area

### 2.1 Map screen: hierarchy and glanceability

**Good**

- The map really does dominate. Chrome is limited to a top-left compass and a right-hand rail of 4–5 round buttons (`01-ios-map-idle`).
- The scale bar sits in the cartographic bottom-left corner and stacks above the recording bar and weather dock instead of colliding with them (`MapScreen.tsx:2250-2260`). That's a thoughtful fix.
- The route line is warm orange-red with a dark casing (`theme.ts:219-226`), which is the correct idiom (Gaia/AllTrails).

**Problems**

- **Record is buried.** The old bottom-right FAB was moved into the top-right rail as a "+" that opens a sheet (`MapActionsMenu.tsx`, doc comment "wave A item 6"). Record is row 1 of that sheet, with a **`timer-outline`** icon (`MapActionsMenu.tsx`, `row('timer-outline','Record track',…)`), so a stopwatch stands for recording. The reason for the move was sound: the FAB was where your thumb pans the map. But the fix put the app's primary verb in the least reachable spot on a 6.9" phone, behind an ambiguous "+". Strava, Komoot and Gaia all keep Record in the bottom thumb zone as a large, labelled, dedicated control.
- **The rail's icons are unlabelled and ambiguous.** Mountain = base map, checkerboard = overlays, and "+" = five unrelated actions (record, waypoint, download, make a map, coordinates). The 3D/download buttons come and go between builds (Android shows a download FAB; iOS doesn't). All five are **40 dp** (`FAB size="small"`, `MapControlsRail.tsx:87-94`), below 44 pt/48 dp.
- **The compass shows "--"** in every screenshot (`CompassBadge.tsx`). With no heading, a 70×90 px tile shows two dashes. When the heading is unknown, collapse it to a small north arrow. The heading text is `fontSize: 10` with `allowFontScaling={false}` (`CompassBadge.tsx:183, 240`).
- **The Android PDF-overlay shot shows two "locate" buttons** (3D main: top-right plus a lime one bottom-left) and a light-blue "Record" pill with a navy dot (`play/05-pdf-overlay-raw`). The newer iOS build fixed some of this, but it shows how chrome accumulates per feature.
- **MapLibre logo plus "i" attribution** sit on top of content in the trail viewer (`02-ios-trail-detail`). Attribution is required, but it can go smaller and further into the corner, as Apple Maps does.

### 2.2 Live recording HUD (from code)

`StatsHud.tsx`:

- Collapsed pill: duration in `titleSmall` (14 pt) and distance in `bodyMedium` (14 pt) (`:88-92`). Expanded card: `maxWidth: 200` (`:174`), six `StatTile compact` with **14 pt values** and **11 pt uppercase labels** (`StatTile.tsx:339-345`). At arm's length on a trail that's too small. Gaia and Strava use 28–40 pt primaries.
- The collapse button is **24×24** (`:186-190`). The expanded record buttons are **38 dp** (`RecordControls.tsx:23`).
- **Status colours fail.** "PAUSED" and "Weak GPS" use `theme.colors.tertiary` = `#AECCDF` on a near-white surface, which is **1.58 : 1** (`StatsHud.tsx:53, 63`). The Pause glyph is `#AECCDF` on `elevation.level3`, **1.40 : 1** (`RecordControls.tsx:89`). The compass needle is the same, **1.46 : 1** (`CompassBadge.tsx:164`). WCAG non-text contrast needs 3 : 1, and the "Weak GPS" warning is safety information.
- Semantics are good: GPS quality exists, live speed isn't the session average, keep-awake is on. The information is right; the presentation isn't.

### 2.3 Design-system consistency

- **The purple leak (the biggest visual bug).** `lightTheme`/`darkTheme` spread `MD3LightTheme.colors` and override only primary/secondary/tertiary/background/surface/surfaceVariant/onSurfaceVariant/outline/error (`theme.ts:18-76`). They inherit `elevation.level0-5` (lavender `rgb(243,237,246)` in light, plum `rgb(44,40,49)` in dark), `outlineVariant`, `inverseSurface`, `surfaceDisabled`, `backdrop` and so on. The tab bar uses `theme.colors.elevation.level2` (`app/(tabs)/_layout.tsx:27`), and Paper's `FAB variant="surface"`, `Searchbar`, `Surface`, bottom sheets and Appbar use elevation colours. You can see it in `01`: lavender tab bar and FABs over a cream app. In `05`: lavender search field, grey-blue "Download" buttons. In `06`: purple-black chrome over an olive/cream brand. The website (`docs/index.html`: `#2d3740`, `#f2ece0`, `#b6c98a`, `#1a230b`) doesn't have this problem.
- **Four colour systems.** MD3 themes (`theme.ts`); `edgePill` (`theme.ts:201-206`); `weatherChrome` (`weather/weatherChrome.ts`, its own dark slab and accent); `mapColors` (`theme.ts:222-233`). Plus hex literals in 25 files, led by `mapLayers.tsx` (10), `MapMakerEditor.tsx` (9), `WaypointMarkerPin.tsx` (5), `Trail3DGLScreen.tsx` (5) and `ElevationProfile.tsx` (`PACE_COLOR #4E97C9`, `HR_COLOR #E0526A`, `TOGGLE_ACTIVE #6FB1DC`, `:98-101`).
- **Three "app styles"** (classic/minimal/edge, `theme.ts:86-214`, Settings > App style) give **six themes** to QA, and styles fork component trees (`MapControlsRail.tsx:50-68`, `EdgeRail`). For a one-person team that's a large maintenance cost for a feature few users will change. It also dilutes the identity: which one is Inukshuk?
- **Typography has no scale beyond Paper defaults.** 56 raw `fontSize` literals: 12 (×13), 11 (×12), 15, 13, 10, 9 (×5), 8, 19, 14. Concentrated in weather, overlays menu, map maker and marine. No brand typeface. `tabular-nums` is used well in StatTile.
- **No spacing or radius tokens.** Gaps of 3/4/6/10/12 and radii of 16/18 are hand-picked per file.

### 2.4 Navigation structure and tabs

- The iOS build has 5 tabs: **Map · Library · Search · Dashboard · Settings** (`app/(tabs)/_layout.tsx:46-59`).
- **"Search" isn't search.** It renders `StoreScreen` (`app/(tabs)/search.tsx`), a map catalogue ("Around you", "Download"). On a map app, users expect Search to find places, peaks, trailheads and coordinates. There is no place search anywhere (no geocoder in `src/`). Rename it for what it is ("Maps" / "Get maps") and put place search on the map.
- **Settings as a permanent tab** uses 20% of the bar on something opened monthly. Outdoor peers put it behind a profile/gear entry.
- **"Dashboard"** is a generic label for personal history (calendar, lifetime totals, activity graph). "Activity" or "Logbook" fits the product's voice better.
- The Android test build shows only 3 tabs (Map, Library, Settings), so the two platforms currently differ in IA.

### 2.5 Library organisation (`03-ios-library`, Android library shots)

**Good:** folders ("bundles"), category colour stripe (`LibraryScreen.tsx:794`), drag-to-folder, one shared confirm flow for destructive deletes (`:70-95`), two distinct empty states for a search that found nothing vs. an empty library (`:1198`).

**Problems:**

- **Every row truncates its most useful data**, e.g. `↑1068 m · ↓1…` and `↑340 m · ↓34…` (iOS). On Android, the title "ultra du bo…" is cut to make room for stats. Five stats on one `labelSmall` line don't fit.
- **No visual identity per item.** Tracks show a generic activity glyph, with no route-shape thumbnail (Strava, Komoot and Gaia all use one, and it's the fastest way to recognise a trip). Maps show a generic map icon and codes like "8CV24 (3 pages)".
- **Edit affordances are always visible.** Every row has a drag grip (`LibraryScreen.tsx:253-260`), a chart button and a ⋮ menu. Every folder header has pencil and trash. That's four icons per row of chrome, and the folder **trash sits one tap away** at the same weight as rename.
- **The "Import" extended FAB overlaps** the next folder's pencil/trash and a row's chart button (`03`, bottom).
- **Mixed content types in one list** (maps, trails, waypoints) with no type filter besides a dialog. Waypoint rows show "Waypoint 1 / Waypoint 2" default names.
- `LibraryScreen.tsx` is **1,425 lines** and `MapScreen.tsx` is **2,572 lines**. That's a design-velocity problem as much as a code one: every visual change touches a monolith.

### 2.6 Trail detail and elevation (`02`)

- The header card (lavender, from the leak) packs name + 5 stats into 14 pt, which is readable. The stats repeat directly below (`↑1068 m ↓1040 m` again).
- **Two instructional lines for one gesture**: "Drag the profile to move the marker on the map." and "Touch the graph to read elevation." (`Trail3DGLScreen.tsx:1041`, `ElevationProfile.tsx:439`). Merge them, or drop both after first use.
- **The chart carries four encodings without a legend.** Elevation is stroked by grade colour (blue→green→orange→red), there's a pale-blue pace curve on an unlabeled second axis, a dashed avg-pace line labelled only "14:58", and note markers. The pace curve crosses the elevation curve constantly.
- **Red is overloaded app-wide**: route line, descent (D-), steepest grade, stop button, HR curve, error. A red segment on the profile could mean steep, descending, or the route.
- The notes list is good, but edit and trash are full-size and side by side on every row.

### 2.7 Offline download (`04`) and map maker

- **This is the best-designed screen.** Base-map thumbnails with a selected check, a three-step quality segment, and a live "≈ 22 tiles · 396 KB" estimate with clear Cancel/Download. The Inukshuk-glyph pin shows up here. The brand should be this visible everywhere.
- Issues: the segmented control's selected fill is grey-blue (`secondaryContainer #D6DBE0`), not brand. "Relief" looks disabled rather than unselected. The corner handles are 20 px dots, small for gloves.
- Map maker (`05`): a clean preview with a "1:5,000 · 10 m contours · A4" caption, which is excellent. The sheet has a lot of dead space below three switches, and the lavender sheet again.

### 2.8 Store / catalogue (`05`)

- Rows read like database dumps: "Burntland Pond OE W, Maine — US Topo / ≈ 105.95 km · USGS US Topo · 24 MB · 2024-04-16 · EN". Distance has **two decimals** (use "106 km"). The metadata wraps mid-token ("24 / MB"). Thumbnails are near-identical locator sketches.
- "Around you" shows Maine quads to a user near Québec. That's correct (nearest USGS), but it would feel less odd with a coverage caption ("Nearest USGS quads, 105 km away") or by showing Canadian sources first.

### 2.9 Empty, error and loading states

- These exist and are honest: store spinner + text (`StoreScreen.tsx:278-313`), library "No maps yet" and "Empty folder" (`LibraryScreen.tsx:1023, 1172`), timed snackbars for outcomes, ErrorBoundary, GPS weak/lost chip.
- They're plain `ActivityIndicator` + `bodyMedium` text, with no illustration, no brand, and no primary action (e.g. "Import a GPX" / "Browse maps"). There are no skeleton rows. The Android trail viewer's disabled "Add note" and the back arrow in dark mode are almost invisible (`android/shot-trail-3d`, top-left).

### 2.10 Iconography

- The Material Community Icons set is consistent. But the product's own metaphors are generic: `timer-outline` for record, `checkerboard/gradient` for overlays, `image-filter-hdr` for base map.
- `InukshukIcon` exists (`src/features/map/components/InukshukIcon.tsx`) and is used for waypoints. **This is your best brand asset in the UI, and it's used in exactly one place.**

### 2.11 Colour, contrast, sunlight and night

- **Good:** onSurfaceVariant pushed to ~15:1 (`theme.ts:39-44`) for outdoor readability, and a contrast test suite (`theme.test.ts:26-44`).
- **Gaps:**
  - The test gates only `onX` on `X` pairs. It never tests an accent colour used as ink on a surface, so `tertiary #AECCDF` ships as foreground at **1.4–1.6 : 1** in the HUD, compass, pause icon and edge tab tint (`tabBarStyle.ts:299`, **1.57 : 1**).
  - The route line `#E8612C` on cream paper is **2.89 : 1** without its casing.
  - The olive location dot `#566B33` over sage forest fill is **3.89 : 1**, easy to lose in woods. A blue or white-ringed puck is the convention for good reason.
- **No night / red-light mode.** Nothing matches `night|red.?light` in `src/`. For an app used at dawn starts, bivouacs and night trail runs, dark mode (`#12150F` background, a pastel UI) isn't night mode.
- **No sunlight mode.** The pastel "Edge" style is the opposite of what bright snow and sun need.

### 2.12 Accessibility

- **Good:** 154 `accessibilityLabel`s across ~67 raw pressables plus Paper buttons. Labels are stable and Maestro-keyed. There's a keyboard Done bar and a destructive-confirm flow.
- **Targets:** rail FABs 40 dp, expanded record buttons 38 dp, HUD collapse 24 dp, note badges 24 dp (`NoteNumberBadge.tsx:10`), download-box handles about 20 dp. Fixes cost almost nothing (`hitSlop`) but aren't systematic.
- **Dynamic type:** Paper `Text` scales by default, but the fixed-height pill/card HUD (`maxWidth: 200`) and the compass (`allowFontScaling={false}`) will clip or ignore it. The 56 raw font sizes aren't tied to a scale that could clamp with `maxFontSizeMultiplier`.
- **Colour-only meaning:** activity category is a 4 px left stripe (`LibraryScreen.tsx:794`), and grade is conveyed by stroke colour. Both need a second channel (icon, pattern, or text on scrub, which grade already has).

### 2.13 Platform conventions

- iOS: centred large title "Library" and the tab bar are fine. Material segmented buttons, the Android-style extended FAB ("+ Import") and pill "Download" buttons read as Android-on-iOS. That's acceptable for a Paper app, but the FAB overlapping content is not.
- Android: the older test build is 3 tabs versus 5 on iOS. Its dark chrome is plum (elevation leak). Its "Record" pill is `tertiary` light blue, which on Android reads as a disabled/secondary tonal button.

### 2.14 Brand identity

- The icon is excellent and specific. It's a charcoal inukshuk on cream paper with contour lines, a slate-blue river and sage slopes, and it reads as boreal, cartographic and quiet. The website carries it well ("Your maps. Your trails. Off the grid.").
- **The app doesn't.** What a user actually sees is a generic MD3 template with an olive primary, lavender chrome and system fonts. The charcoal "stone" (`#2D3740`) is relegated to `secondary`, which almost nothing uses. There's no contour texture, no stone-stack motif and no typographic voice. The pastel "Edge" style drifts further from the brand.

## 3. Proposals (ranked)

### Proposal 1: **Trail Mode** (thumb-zone record and a glanceable instrument strip)

**Kind:** Map/HUD glanceability · **Effort:** M (≈1–1.5 wk) · **Impact:** Very high (core in-field use and safety)

**Problem, with evidence**

- Record is two taps deep in the top-right "+" sheet with a stopwatch icon (`MapActionsMenu.tsx` rows, `MapControlsRail.tsx:115-122`).
- HUD values are 14 pt, labels 11 pt, and the card is at most 200 px wide (`StatsHud.tsx:88-92, 119-141, 174`; `StatTile.tsx:343`).
- Status ink is 1.4–1.6:1 (`StatsHud.tsx:53,63`; `RecordControls.tsx:89`). Targets are 24–40 dp.
- No store screenshot shows recording, which suggests the screen isn't yet one you'd want to show off.

**Design**

1. **Idle: a dedicated Record button in the thumb zone.** A 64 dp circular button, bottom-centre, 16 dp above the tab bar. Stone charcoal `#2D3740` fill, a white **filled circle** glyph (the universal record mark, not a timer), and a "Record" label under it (12 pt, on a translucent paper chip). "+" stays in the rail for the secondary actions (waypoint, download, make map, coordinates) and loses Record. Map pan gestures still work around it: the button is 64 dp in a ~390 pt wide viewport, so the earlier "thumb pans there" concern is met by keeping it _centred and small_, not a corner FAB.
2. **Recording: the instrument strip.** A full-width bottom card (docked above the tab bar, or replacing it while recording, as Strava does) with **three user-configurable fields**:
   - Values in 32 pt / weight 700 / tabular-nums, labels 12 pt / 600 / uppercase with `onSurfaceVariant`.
   - Default fields are _Time · Distance · Elevation gain_. Tap a field to cycle it (speed, pace, altitude, D-, distance-to-destination, time-to-sunset).
   - A swipe-up detent reveals the second row (the current 6 tiles, at 20 pt) plus the elevation-so-far sparkline.
3. **The status rail is the top edge of the strip.** It's a 4 dp colour bar plus a text chip. Recording = stone + a pulsing red dot. **Paused = amber `#B45309` bar + "PAUSED" chip, filling the whole strip hatch.** GPS weak = amber chip "Weak GPS · ±35 m". GPS lost = red chip "No GPS · 2 min". Every state is carried by **text and shape**, never tint alone.
4. **Controls are big and separated.** Pause/Resume is a 56 dp primary. Waypoint (Inukshuk glyph) is 56 dp. **Stop is long-press 800 ms with a ring fill** (it prevents pocket stops and lets you skip the confirm dialog). Keep all current `accessibilityLabel`s ("Stop recording", "Pause", "Resume", "Add waypoint") so Maestro flows keep passing.
5. **Glove lock (optional toggle in the strip's overflow).** It disables map pan/zoom gestures and all buttons except long-press unlock. It's the Garmin/Gaia "lock screen" idea.

**Wireframe (before → after)**

**Implementation touch-points**

- `src/features/map/components/StatsHud.tsx`: rewrite as `InstrumentStrip` (collapsed/expanded detents, a `fields` prop).
- `src/ui/components/StatTile.tsx`: add a `size: 'hero' | 'standard' | 'compact'` variant (32/20/14 pt).
- `src/features/map/components/RecordControls.tsx`: 56 dp buttons, long-press stop with a progress ring, and a new status palette (from Proposal 2 tokens).
- `src/features/map/components/MapActionsMenu.tsx`: remove the "Record track" row, **or** keep it as a duplicate so Maestro `record.yaml` still passes, then migrate the flow.
- `src/features/map/MapScreen.tsx:2257-2300`: bottom-column layout, the idle Record button, and hiding the tab bar while recording (`navigation.setOptions({ tabBarStyle: { display: 'none' } })`).
- `src/state/settingsStore.ts`: `hudFields: [FieldId, FieldId, FieldId]` and `gloveLock: boolean`.
- `.maestro/record.yaml`: update taps. Add a **store screenshot of recording**.

**Risks and trade-offs**

- A permanent bottom-centre button covers some map at idle. You can mitigate it: auto-hide after 4 s of panning and reappear on stop.
- Hiding the tab bar while recording is a mode users must understand. Show a "Recording, tap to return" pill if they navigate away via deep link.
- Configurable fields add settings surface. Ship the 3 defaults first and add cycling later.

### Proposal 2: **Stone & Paper tokens** (fix the purple leak, one semantic layer, real brand moments)

**Kind:** Design system / brand · **Effort:** S for step A, M for B–D · **Impact:** High (every screen, and it unlocks P1, P3 and P4)

**Problem, with evidence**

- Inherited MD3 lavender/plum `elevation.*` and `outlineVariant` (`theme.ts:18-76`) paint the tab bar (`app/(tabs)/_layout.tsx:27`), FABs, the compass, sheets and the search field. See `01`, `02`, `04`, `05`, `06`.
- Four colour systems (`theme.ts`, `edgePill`, `weatherChrome.ts`, `mapColors`) plus ~70 literals in 25 files, 56 raw font sizes and 6 themes.
- The brand's charcoal stone (`#2D3740`) is effectively unused, and the icon's contour-paper character is absent.

**Design**

- **A. Stop the leak (a 1-day fix, ship it first).** In all six themes, override `elevation.level0-5` with warm paper steps and override `outlineVariant`, `surfaceDisabled`, `onSurfaceDisabled`, `inverseSurface`, `inverseOnSurface`, `inversePrimary`, `backdrop` and `shadow`.
  - Light: `level0 transparent · level1 #F7F2E8 · level2 #F3EDE1 · level3 #EEE7D9 · level4 #ECE4D5 · level5 #E8E0CF`. `outlineVariant #D5CEBF`.
  - Dark (stone night, from the icon's charcoal): `background #13171B · surface #1A1F24 · level1 #1F252B · level2 #242B32 · level3 #29313A · level4 #2B343D · level5 #2F3842`. `outlineVariant #3E4852`.
  - Add a test that asserts that **no** colour in any theme has a hue in 250–300° with saturation > 8%, so the purple can't return.
- **B. One token module, `src/ui/tokens.ts`**, with three layers.
  1. `palette`: raw brand hues from the icon: stone `#2D3740`, paper `#F2ECE0`, sage `#93A25E` / deep `#566B33`, river `#5C93B7`, granite `#8A8B8C`, ochre `#C98A2B`, signal red `#C62828`.
  2. `semantic`:
     - `map.chrome.{bg, ink, inkMuted, active}`: this is where `edgePill` and `weatherChrome` merge. There's one "slab over tiles" look: stone at 92% with paper ink.
     - `status.{recording, paused, gpsWeak, gpsLost, success}`.
     - `data.{route, routeCasing, overlay, overlayActive, ascent, descent, pace, hr, grade[5]}`.
     - `category.{hike, run, ski, bike, paddle}`.
  3. `scale`:
     - `space` 4/8/12/16/24/32.
     - `radius` 8/12/16/28.
     - `type`: display 32, title 20, body 16, label 13, caption 12. **Nothing below 12.**
     - `target.min` 48.
  - Re-express the MD3 themes from these tokens. Replace every hex literal (`grep -rE "'#[0-9A-Fa-f]{3,8}'" src --include=*.tsx`) and every raw `fontSize` with tokens, and add an ESLint `no-restricted-syntax` rule for hex literals outside `src/ui/`.
- **C. Collapse app styles from 3 to 1 (plus density).** Keep **Classic** and make it the brand. Replace "Minimal" with a **"Compact map chrome" toggle** (fold the rail), which is the only behaviour it adds. ### Proposal 3: **Sun, Night & Gloves** (outdoor-conditions and accessibility pass)

**Kind:** Accessibility / outdoor conditions · **Effort:** M · **Impact:** High (safety and legibility in real use)

**Problem, with evidence**

- Accent-as-ink fails: `tertiary #AECCDF` on light surfaces is 1.40–1.58:1 (`StatsHud.tsx:53,63`, `RecordControls.tsx:89`, `CompassBadge.tsx:164`, `TrailViewerRail.tsx:37`, `tabBarStyle.ts:299` for edge). `theme.test.ts:38-44` never checks it.
- Targets below 44/48: `FAB size="small"` (40) across the rail (`MapControlsRail.tsx:58-103`, `MapActionsMenu.tsx`), 38 dp record buttons (`RecordControls.tsx:23`), a 24 dp HUD collapse (`StatsHud.tsx:186`), 24 dp note badges, ~20 dp region handles (`04`).
- No night/red mode and no sunlight mode (no matches in `src/`). The compass opts out of font scaling (`CompassBadge.tsx:183`). Categories are colour-only (`LibraryScreen.tsx:794`). Red is overloaded (route, descent, steep grade, stop, HR, error).

**Design**

1. **Contrast gates that match real usage.** Add a `usage` matrix to `theme.test.ts`: every `(fg token, bg token)` pair the code actually renders, including `status.*` on `surface`/`elevation.level3`, `data.route` on `paper`, and `map.chrome.ink` on `map.chrome.bg`. Text needs ≥ 4.5:1 and icons/graphics ≥ 3:1. Replace `tertiary` as ink with `status.paused = #B45309` (amber-700, 4.7:1 on paper) and `status.gpsWeak = #B45309` plus text, and use the stone ink for the compass needle with a red north tip.
2. **Sunlight theme** (auto-on while recording, if the user opts in):
   - Pure `#FFFFFF` surfaces, `#000000` ink, 2 px outlines on all map chrome, and HUD values up one step (32 → 40 pt).
   - Route line at 6 px with a 2 px white casing plus a dark outer casing (the triple-casing Gaia uses), and basemap `raster-contrast: 0.15`.
   - Toggle it from a sun icon in the instrument strip's overflow and in Settings > Display.
3. **Night theme (red light):**
   - The UI uses tokens `bg #000000`, `ink #FF3B30` at 60–100%, and `muted #7A1C17`, with **no blue or green anywhere**, so the screen doesn't destroy dark adaptation.
   - For the map, apply `raster-saturation: -1` and `raster-brightness-max: 0.35` on raster basemaps and PDF overlays, dim vector layers via a night style variant, and add a full-screen `rgba(120,0,0,0.35)` overlay View (`pointerEvents="none"`) to red-shift what remains.
   - Be honest about it in Settings. Point to **iOS Accessibility › Display › Color Filters › Colour Tint (red)** and the Android equivalent for a true red-only screen, and offer to open system settings.
   - Entry points: a moon icon in the map overflow, plus auto-schedule at local sunset if the user opts in (you already have the location and date for weather).
4. **Gloves: a 48 dp floor, enforced.** Add a `HitTarget` wrapper (min 48×48 via `hitSlop` when the visual is smaller). Swap `FAB size="small"` to a custom 48 dp `MapButton` (40 dp visual + 4 dp slop, or 48 dp visual in Sunlight). Enlarge the region-select handles to 32 dp visual and 48 dp hit. Add an ESLint rule or test that scans for `size="small"` on FABs in `src/features/map`.
5. **Dynamic type.**
   - Remove `allowFontScaling={false}` from the compass and let the heading readout reflow.
   - Give HUD numbers `maxFontSizeMultiplier={1.4}` and make the strip grow vertically, never clip.
   - Test at iOS AX3 / Android 200% in a Maestro flow (`settings.yaml` already exists; add a font-scale variant).
6. **Meaning beyond colour.**
   - Category: stripe + activity glyph (already there) + label in the accessibility string.
   - Elevation profile: add a compact legend chip row (`Grade · Pace · Avg 14:58/km`) and give the pace curve a dashed stroke so it's distinguishable without hue.
   - **Red reserved for danger/stop/lost**. Descent becomes `granite #5F6B76` with a ↓ glyph. The steepest grade band becomes `deep purple-brown #6D2E46`, or keep red but never use red for descent.

**Wireframe (night mode, recording)**

```
┌──────────────────────────────┐   all ink #FF3B30 on #000
│[N]                      (◎) │   map: desaturated, brightness≤35%, red veil
│   ░░░░░░ map, dim red ░░░░░  │
│   ░░░░ ━━━━ route ━━━ ░░░░░  │   route: #FF3B30 6px, no casing
│ ┌──────────────────────────┐ │
│ │ ● REC · GPS ±5 m         │ │
│ │ 1:02:14   3.42 km  +268 m│ │   32pt
│ │ [ ❚❚ ]   [ ⛯ ]    (■)hold│ │   56dp
│ └──────────────────────────┘ │
│  ☾ Night on · tap to exit    │
└──────────────────────────────┘
```

**Implementation touch-points**

- `src/ui/theme.ts` + `src/ui/tokens.ts`: `sunlightLight`, `nightRed` derived themes, and `resolveTheme(style, scheme, condition)`.
- `src/ui/theme.test.ts`: usage-pair matrix.
- New `src/ui/components/HitTarget.tsx` and `MapButton.tsx`, used in `MapControlsRail.tsx`, `MapActionsMenu.tsx`, `RecordControls.tsx`, `StatsHud.tsx`, `RegionSelectOverlay.tsx` (handles), `NoteNumberBadge.tsx`.
- `src/features/map/mapLayers.tsx`: raster paint props per condition, and a night vector style variant.
- `src/features/map/components/CompassBadge.tsx:164,183,240`.
- `src/features/common/components/ElevationProfile.tsx:98-101, 255, 439`: legend, dashed pace, grade palette.
- `src/features/library/LibraryScreen.tsx:794`.
- `src/state/settingsStore.ts`: `condition: 'auto' | 'normal' | 'sunlight' | 'night'` and `nightAtSunset`.
- `.maestro/settings.yaml`: font-scale and night variants.

**Risks and trade-offs**

- An in-app red overlay isn't a true red-only display. Blue and green subpixels still emit, so say so and link to the OS filter.
- Sunlight auto-switching can surprise people. Make it opt-in and show a toast on switch.
- More theme variants mean more QA, which is why P2 step C (dropping Edge/Minimal) should land first. Net theme count: 6 → 4 (light, dark, sunlight, night).

### Proposal 4: **Library as a logbook, plus honest tabs**

**Kind:** IA / library flows · **Effort:** M–L · **Impact:** Medium-high (daily use, first impression, store conversion)

**Problem, with evidence**

- Rows truncate their key stats (`03`: "↑1068 m · ↓1…"; Android: "ultra du bo…"). Items have no visual identity (generic glyphs, no route thumbnails). Four always-on edit icons per row, and a folder trash one tap away (`LibraryScreen.tsx:253-260`, folder headers). The Import FAB overlaps content.
- The "Search" tab is actually the map store (`app/(tabs)/search.tsx`), and there's no place search anywhere. Settings takes a whole tab. "Dashboard" is generic. iOS has 5 tabs and Android 3.
- The store rows are metadata dumps with over-precise distance (`05`: "≈ 105.95 km").

**Design**

1. **Tabs: Map · Library · Maps · Logbook** (4 tabs, the same on both platforms).
   - _Maps_ = today's StoreScreen, retitled "Get maps", with a search field _for maps_.
   - _Logbook_ = today's Dashboard.
   - **Settings** moves to a gear in the Library and Logbook headers (and stays deep-linkable).
   - Add **place search on the Map**: a search pill top-centre that expands to a sheet (peaks, lakes, trailheads, coordinates). Reuse the existing `GoToCoordinatesDialog` for coordinates, and start offline-first with names from downloaded vector tiles and PDF labels, with an online geocoder when there's signal.
2. **Library top bar:** title, a gear, and a sort/filter icon. Under it, a **segmented chip row: All · Trails · Maps · Waypoints** with counts, and a search field that appears on pull-down.
3. **Trail row, two lines, never truncated:**
   - A 56×56 **route thumbnail**: the polyline drawn in `data.route` on paper with a contour texture. Generate it once at import/record time with `react-native-svg` from the simplified track and cache it.
   - **Line 1:** name (titleSmall, 1 line).
   - **Line 2:** `11.2 km · 3:31 · ↑1068 m`, three stats only, with pace and descent in detail view.
   - **Line 3 (caption):** `Aug 29 · Hike`.
   - The whole row opens the trail. Long-press or ⋮ holds everything else (show on map, profile, move, rename, category, Strava, delete).
   - The chart button leaves the row, because opening the trail already shows the profile.
4. **Map row:** a thumbnail of page 1 (you already rasterise PDFs), a human title (strip codes, show "Charlevoix · 3 pages" with the code as caption), and an "On map" toggle chip instead of a chevron.
5. **Organize mode.** Drag grips, folder rename and folder delete appear only after tapping "Organize" in the header. That's Apple Files/Photos behaviour, and it removes ~40% of row chrome. Folder delete moves into an overflow with the existing confirm flow.
6. **Import.** It becomes a header "+" menu (Import GPX / Import PDF map / New folder), or the extended FAB gets `contentContainerStyle` bottom padding of FAB height + 16 so nothing hides under it.
7. **Store rows.** Title "Burntland Pond (West)". Caption `USGS US Topo · 24 MB · 106 km away`, with rounded distance and date/language in detail. The primary "Download" uses stone fill, and "Downloaded ✓" uses a sage tonal state. A section note: "Nearest USGS quads. Canadian sources ▸".
8. **Empty states with a primary action and brand art:**
   - Library: contour texture + Inukshuk glyph + "No trails yet" + [Record a trail] [Import GPX].
   - Maps: "No maps downloaded" + [Browse maps near you].

**Implementation touch-points**

- New `src/features/map/components/PlaceSearch.tsx`, with a label index from downloaded tiles (a later phase).

**Risks and trade-offs**

- Moving Settings out of the tab bar reduces discoverability for power users. Mitigate it with a gear in two headers and a "Settings" row in the "+" sheet.
- Route thumbnails cost CPU at import. Generate them lazily and cache them. The same `simplify` exists in core.
- Renaming tabs breaks muscle memory for current iOS users. Small base, early product: do it now rather than later.
- Offline place search is a real feature, not a UI tweak. Ship the tab rename immediately and stage search.
